What is The Emoji Spin?

The Emoji Spin is a fast-paced, multiplayer dare game. Forget complex rules — just select your player count, hit the center button, and let the wheel decide your fate. Perfect for couples, friends at a sleepover, or breaking the ice on a first date.

How to Play

  1. Select Players: Choose 2, 3, or 4 players using the slider above the wheel.
  2. Press Spin: Click the big yellow button in the center of the wheel.
  3. Wait for the stop: The wheel will spin and slow down.
  4. Read the screen: Look at the digital display above the wheel to see WHO has to do WHAT.
  5. Do the dare: Whatever the screen says, you have to do it!
