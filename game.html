How to Play

  1. Choose who performs: Slide the toggle above the slot machine to pick "Boy does it" or "Girl does it."
  2. Get ready: The boy holds the blue button (or key A), the girl holds the pink button (or key L).
  3. Press together: Both players press within 400 milliseconds of each other. If only one presses, nothing happens.
  4. See the dare: The slot lands on one emoji and shows a dare — for example, "Kiss me on the cheeks" or "Hold my hand."
  5. Do it: The chosen player performs the dare. Press together again for the next round.
  6. Don't chicken out: If you can't do the dare, you lose the round. No excuses.

Playing on a PC? Use the Keyboard

On a desktop or laptop, two people can play using the keyboard. Player 1 presses A, Player 2 presses L — both at the same time. The same 400-millisecond window applies.
